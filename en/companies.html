General legal advice (external in-house counsel)

We advise on legal questions arising in running your business, for example in employment, contract, sale and lease law, including international matters with our network partners. We help you comply with corporate law, conclude shareholders' agreements and register, maintain and enforce your intellectual property rights.

M&A, corporate law, structures and corporate finance

We support acquisitions of and cooperations with other companies, prepare board and shareholders' meetings and structure your areas of activity. As a sounding board we support your management and board of directors and help you find the right structure and financing.

Debt collection, enforcement and composition proceedings

We collect outstanding invoices and represent your company in debt enforcement and composition proceedings before the competent authorities.

Formation, restructuring and liquidation

We have long experience in forming and liquidating legal entities and support you in structural or financial restructurings.

Board of directors and corporate secretary

We are happy to serve as members or secretaries of the board of directors and contribute our know-how to the strategic management of your company. As corporate secretary we ensure that your company is run in compliance with Swiss law and carefully take the minutes of board and shareholders' meetings.

Administrative, court and arbitration proceedings

We represent your company before Swiss courts and authorities and in national and international arbitrations.

Business consulting, accounting, finance and tax

With our local partners and our national and international network, we also offer your company accounting, tax advice and general business consulting.
