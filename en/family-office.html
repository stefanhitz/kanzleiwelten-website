Relocation, authorities and administration

We organise your new home in Switzerland and take care of your registration with the authorities. During your stay we assist you in administrative matters and are in contact with the relevant offices.

We advise you on the establishment, structure and administration of trusts and foundations, so that your assets can be protected and used according to your wishes over generations. We are also happy to act as foundation board members, trustees or protectors.

Through our network we offer interesting investments in art and real estate and advise – partly through partners – on tax, customs, inheritance and insurance matters. We also support you with the storage, trading, lending and maintenance of your art or classic car collection.

Legal and tax
