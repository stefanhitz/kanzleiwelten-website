About us

Quality and discretion are the cornerstones of our work.

Kanzlei Welten was founded at the beginning of 2017 by Bernhard Welten, attorney-at-law, LL.M. We offer services in law, tax, finance and accounting as well as board memberships and corporate secretarial services. Together with external experts we also provide notarial and asset management services.

Thanks to our long-standing membership in TAG Alliances, an international network of medium-sized law and accounting firms, and a Swiss network of law firms, we provide our services not only regionally and throughout Switzerland but worldwide. Our geographical focus is the greater Berne area and Gstaad/Saanenland, with a strong international orientation.

High-quality services, provided discreetly: this is the basis of our often long-standing client relationships – built on mutual trust, competence, discretion and high ethical standards.

Founder

Bernhard Welten, attorney-at-law, LL.M.

Bernhard Welten has practised law since 1996, from 2000 as a partner in a medium-sized business law firm in Berne. He founded Kanzlei Welten at the beginning of 2017.

  • Studies at the University of Berne, bar exam 1995
  • LL.M. 1999, Duke University School of Law (North Carolina, USA), focus on commercial law and corporate finance
  • More than 100 arbitrations as sole arbitrator, party-appointed arbitrator or chair, including at the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS)
  • Member of the Bernese and Swiss Bar Associations, registered with the Bar Registry of the Canton of Berne
