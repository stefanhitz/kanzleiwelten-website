Private individuals

Contract law

We draft and review contracts of all kinds, such as employment, sale or work contracts, and support you in negotiations.

Inheritance and succession planning

We advise on succession planning, the settlement of estates and act as executors. We represent you in inheritance disputes and help protect your family assets over generations. Notarial deeds are handled together with notaries throughout Switzerland.

Investments and tax

We advise you on all tax matters and help you structure and secure your investments according to your needs.

Real estate and construction law

Through our network we regularly receive attractive offers for real estate in Switzerland and abroad. We support you in selecting, buying, renting, maintaining, renovating, building, selling or letting property, working with architects, builders and real estate professionals in the greater Berne area and Gstaad/Saanenland.

Litigation, administrative and arbitration proceedings

We assess the chances and risks of potential or pending proceedings and represent you before regional and national courts and authorities; in the French- and Italian-speaking parts of Switzerland together with local attorneys from our network if needed. In arbitration we also work in English.

Companies

General legal advice (external in-house counsel)

We advise on legal questions arising in running your business, for example in employment, contract, sale and lease law, including international matters with our network partners. We help you comply with corporate law, conclude shareholders' agreements and register, maintain and enforce your intellectual property rights.

M&A, corporate law, structures and corporate finance

We support acquisitions and cooperations, prepare board and shareholders' meetings, structure your activities and act as a sounding board for your management and board of directors.

Debt collection and enforcement

We collect outstanding receivables and represent you in debt enforcement and composition proceedings.

Formation, restructuring and liquidation

We have long experience in forming and liquidating legal entities and support you in structural or financial restructurings.

Board of directors and corporate secretary

We serve as members or secretaries of the board of directors, ensure compliance with Swiss law and carefully draft the minutes of board and shareholders' meetings.

Administrative, court and arbitration proceedings

We represent your company before Swiss courts and authorities and in national and international arbitrations.

Accounting, finance and tax

Accounting, payroll and audit

Together with fiduciary partners we offer payroll, accounting, auditing, finance and tax services – on site or with specialists of your choice, nationally and internationally.

Tax advice

Our team includes a certified Swiss tax expert and German tax advisor as well as a certified auditor.

Board functions
