Visiting this website

Our hosting provider Hostpoint AG (Switzerland) automatically stores technical access data in server logs, such as IP address, date and time and the page requested. Contact form and e-mail

If you contact us, we process your details (name, e-mail address, phone number if provided, subject and message) to answer your enquiry. Your data are subject to attorney-client privilege and are deleted once no longer needed, unless the law requires us to keep them. Unencrypted e-mail is not fully protected against access by third parties.
