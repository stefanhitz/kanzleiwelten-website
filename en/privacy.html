Privacy

How we handle your data on this website.

Controller

Kanzlei Welten, as named in the legal notice, is responsible for processing your personal data within the meaning of the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). Please send any questions about data protection to info@kanzleiwelten.com.

Visiting this website

When you visit this website, our hosting provider Hostpoint AG (Switzerland) automatically stores technical access data in server logs, such as IP address, date and time and the page requested. These data are used solely for the secure and trouble-free operation of the website and are not combined with other data.

This website uses no cookies and no analytics or tracking services. Fonts are loaded from our own server; no data are transferred to Google or other third parties.

Contact form and e-mail

If you contact us via the contact form or by e-mail, we process your details (name, e-mail address, phone number if provided, subject and message) in order to answer your enquiry. The form is processed on the servers of Hostpoint AG in Switzerland and forwarded to us by e-mail. Our e-mail is handled via Microsoft 365. Your data are subject to attorney-client privilege and are deleted once your enquiry has been dealt with, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Please note that unencrypted e-mails and forms on the internet are not fully protected against access by third parties. Please therefore send us confidential documents by a secure method agreed with us in advance.

Your rights

You may at any time request information about the personal data we process about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Please contact us at the address given above.
