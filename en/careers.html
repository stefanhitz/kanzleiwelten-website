Careers

Kanzlei Welten is a law firm in Berne with a strong national and international network, focusing on business, contract and sports law. Are you a cheerful, motivated and curious person who enjoys working in a small team? Then you have come to the right place.

Attorney-at-law

Start date by arrangement

You have completed your law degree and hold a Swiss bar admission, express yourself fluently in German, French and English, both orally and in writing, and may already have some initial professional experience.

We offer a dynamic environment with challenging and interesting tasks in a committed and friendly team, complemented by fiduciaries, auditors and tax experts. You will handle your own mandates, mainly in contract, criminal and family law, and represent clients in court. We support you in building up your own client base, and you can count on the know-how and resources of the team.

We look forward to receiving your application by e-mail to Ms Daniela Welten: sekretariat@kanzleiwelten.com

Legal internship (Anwaltspraktikum)

Starting immediately or by arrangement, 9 to 12 months

During the internship you will gain varied insight into the work of an attorney: you will draft and edit briefs, judgments and contracts, appear independently before courts and authorities where possible, carry out legal research and have contact with clients.

You hold a master's degree in law and ideally already have some initial legal experience or have completed a court internship.

We look forward to receiving your application by e-mail to Ms Daniela Welten: sekretariat@kanzleiwelten.com

Legal associate 40–60%

Start date by arrangement

You carry out legal research, help draft briefs, judgments and contracts, appear before courts and authorities where possible and are in contact with our clients.

You preferably hold a bachelor's degree in law and ideally already have some initial legal experience.
